# Shot list: eight camera moves, eight worlds

All characters are AI-generated and fictional. One take per move and per arm, MiniMax H3 (int8 build), same still, same seed (777001 for the production takes), 1152x648 final, 24 fps, 124 frames per H3 take.

| Shot | Move and world | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| w1 | push-in · samurai in night rain | Push-in: a samurai kneels in night rain before a temple gate; the camera pushes in to his hat brim as he lifts his head and his eyes appear. |
| w2 | tilt · jungle colossus | Tilt: from an explorer at the foot of mossy jungle steps, the camera tilts up to reveal a colossal carved stone face above the canopy. |
| w3 | orbit · car under neon rain | Orbit: a gloss black 1970s coupe in a wet neon alley; the camera arcs ~30 deg along its flank, reflections sliding over the bodywork. |
| w4 | pull-out · cabin in a blizzard | Pull-out: a log cabin with one warm window in a blizzard at the end of blue hour; the camera pulls back until it is a small point in the valley. |
| w5 | truck · steam train in the highlands | Truck: a plain black steam train crosses a stone viaduct right to left, side-on; the camera trucks with it, a pine passing in front. |
| w6 | pedestal · rocket on the pad | Pedestal: a plain white rocket at rest on the pad at night; the camera rises straight up past the tower floors and hull bands. |
| w7 | dolly zoom · climber on a cliff | Dolly zoom: a climber on a granite ledge; she stays the same size while the spires behind her swell (vertigo). |
| w8 | crane-up · desert caravan | Crane-up: low on a dune at golden hour; the camera rises as the dune sea opens around a six-camel caravan. |

The grey plan for each move is in `moves/NN-<move>/previs_params.json`; render it with `previs-tools/pv_render.py` in Blender 4.5.
